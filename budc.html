What happens when you come in

  1. We look at the tooth.

    No charge for this. It takes a few minutes and nothing is billed for it.

  2. You hear what it is and what it costs.

    If it needs treatment, we say so, and we hand you the price in writing before anything starts.

  3. You decide.

    Go ahead the same day, or don't. If you'd rather think about it, that's a fine answer and there's no charge for the look either way.

Once you decide to go ahead, that's when the exam and x-ray begin — those are treatment, and they're on the price list below.

What we take care of

  • A tooth that needs to come out
  • Toothache or an abscess
  • A broken tooth, a lost crown, a filling that came out
  • Swelling on one side of the face
  • A denture or partial that broke or won't stay in

What we don't

  • Cleanings and checkups
  • Braces, whitening, veneers
  • Anything that isn't the reason you called
